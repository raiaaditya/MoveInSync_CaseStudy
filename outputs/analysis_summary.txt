C. POSSIBLE EXPLANATIONS (HYPOTHESES - NOT shown by this data)
Public reviews describe symptoms in the reviewers' own words. They cannot prove technical or
operational causes. Treat the items below as questions to investigate with internal data.

* App not loading / launch / crashes / errors  (observed in 28.2% of low-rated reviews)
    Possible contributors (unverified): Release regressions, device/OS incompatibilities, server outages, install or update problems.
    What would confirm it: Check crash analytics by app version/device/OS and incident logs.

* Cab booking / scheduling / allocation  (observed in 25.5% of low-rated reviews)
    Possible contributors (unverified): Vehicle supply gaps, booking cut-off rules set by employers, roster configuration, missing booking features.
    What would confirm it: Analyse the booking-to-allocation funnel and cancellation reasons.

* Tracking / GPS / ETA accuracy  (observed in 21.8% of low-rated reviews)
    Possible contributors (unverified): Delayed or missing location pings from driver phones/vehicle devices, ETA calculation assumptions, map/API behaviour, weak mobile data.
    What would confirm it: Compare app-displayed location/ETA with raw vehicle telemetry and actual arrival times.

* Pickup/drop timing and delays  (observed in 19.5% of low-rated reviews)
    Possible contributors (unverified): Traffic, route planning, vendor/driver availability, last-minute roster changes, ETA recalculation.
    What would confirm it: Compare scheduled vs actual pickup times in trip logs.

* UI / usability / missing features  (observed in 18.2% of low-rated reviews)
    Possible contributors (unverified): Features limited by employer configuration, or hard to discover.
    What would confirm it: Run usability tests and track feature requests.

* Login / OTP / registration / auto-logout  (observed in 15.6% of low-rated reviews)
    Possible contributors (unverified): OTP/SMS/email delivery failures, mismatches with the employer's employee records, session-timeout or single-sign-on policies.
    What would confirm it: Check OTP delivery and authentication logs by device, carrier and employer.

* Notifications / reminders / feedback prompts  (observed in 12.0% of low-rated reviews)
    Possible contributors (unverified): Mandatory rating steps, overlapping channels (push, SMS, email, calls), missing notification settings.
    What would confirm it: Review product configuration and notification logs.

* Routing / pooling / pickup points  (observed in 10.5% of low-rated reviews)
    Possible contributors (unverified): Route-optimisation trade-offs (pooling vs trip duration), pickup points configured by employers.
    What would confirm it: Review route and trip-duration data.

* Support / helpdesk / complaint handling  (observed in 9.4% of low-rated reviews)
    Possible contributors (unverified): Staffing levels, unclear hand-offs between the employer's transport team and the vendor, slow escalation.
    What would confirm it: Review ticket response/resolution times and contact-channel data.

* Mentions an update / new version  (observed in 8.6% of low-rated reviews)
    Possible contributors (unverified): Regressions or UI changes in recent releases. Note: this is what reviewers SAY; the timing is not verified here.
    What would confirm it: Compare ratings and crash rates before and after each release.

* Driver / staff conduct  (observed in 4.5% of low-rated reviews)
    Possible contributors (unverified): Vendor driver training and monitoring, coordination gaps between rider and driver.
    What would confirm it: Review trip feedback and complaint tickets by vendor.

* Calls, call quality and app permissions  (observed in 4.1% of low-rated reviews)
    Possible contributors (unverified): Company device-management policies, OS permission rules, masked-calling set-up.
    What would confirm it: Test permission flows by OS version and employer policy.

* Safety concerns  (observed in 1.9% of low-rated reviews)
    Possible contributors (unverified): SOS feature reliability, friction in safe-reach confirmations, driver-conduct controls.
    What would confirm it: Review SOS test logs and safety incident reports.

D. LIMITATIONS
- Theme rules are keyword-based. Some reviews are mis-tagged or missed. Check
  outputs/tables/theme_validation_sample.csv by hand before quoting percentages.
- Reviewers are self-selected and may not represent all users.
- Redaction removes emails, phone numbers, links and long ID numbers. It cannot reliably remove
  names. Read the example excerpts before sharing them.
